# Introduction Review Rubric

Use this rubric after drafting or rewriting an Introduction.

## General Science Test

Pass criteria:

- The first paragraph is intelligible to a scientist outside the field.
- The opening problem is broad enough to matter beyond a niche technique or molecule.
- Specialized terms and abbreviations are defined before use.
- The opening answers why the topic matters.

Failure response:

- Replace narrow jargon with a broader problem frame.
- Define necessary terms.
- Move niche details to later paragraphs.

## Knowledge Gap Authenticity Test

Pass criteria:

- The gap is a real unresolved scientific question, contradiction, mechanism, or bottleneck.
- The gap is not merely `X has not been tested in Y`.
- A method upgrade is justified by the new knowledge it enables, not by novelty of the tool alone.
- Prior work is treated as a foundation rather than a straw target.

Failure response:

- Sharpen the gap into a conceptual, mechanistic, causal, translational, or technical barrier.
- Remove inflated claims that prior work failed completely.

## Over-Promising Test

Pass criteria:

- The final paragraph's promise can be fulfilled by the provided Results.
- Claimed mechanism has mechanistic evidence.
- Claimed clinical value has clinical or appropriately bounded translational evidence.
- Claimed broad principle is supported by breadth across models, contexts, or independent validation.

Failure response:

- Replace `resolve`, `establish`, or `demonstrate` with `suggest`, `nominate`, or `provide a framework` when evidence is weaker.
- Bound claims to the tested model, cohort, disease subtype, or assay.

## Citation Sincerity Test

Pass criteria:

- Citations are few, high-value, and directly relevant to the gap.
- Foundational, recent, and conflicting work are represented when relevant.
- Self-citations do not dominate.
- The Introduction does not ignore inconvenient literature.

Failure response:

- Add citation slots for missing foundational or conflicting work.
- Remove low-value citation padding.

## Narrative Momentum Test

Pass criteria:

- Paragraph 1 establishes a broad problem.
- Paragraph 2 narrows to the unresolved gap.
- Paragraph 3 explains why this study can address the gap.
- Paragraph 4 previews the discovery and points toward Results.
- Transitions make the narrowing feel inevitable.

Failure response:

- Reorder paragraphs to restore the funnel.
- Add bridging logic where the topic jumps.
- Cut side issues that do not drive the central question.

## Final Acceptance Standard

Accept the Introduction only if:

- It has broad appeal without sacrificing precision.
- It defines a meaningful and honest gap.
- It presents an original approach.
- It previews a compelling but data-bounded payoff.
- It creates clear momentum into the Results.
