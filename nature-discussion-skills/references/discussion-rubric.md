# Discussion Review Rubric

Use this rubric after drafting or rewriting a Discussion section.

## Over-Claiming Test

Pass criteria:

- Conclusions are bounded by the Results evidence.
- In vitro data are not framed as clinical efficacy.
- Animal data are not framed as human proof.
- Association is not written as direct causation.
- Discovery analyses are framed as nomination, prioritization, or hypothesis generation unless experimentally validated.

Failure response:

- Downgrade claim strength.
- Add the missing validation requirement.
- Replace translational certainty with cautious opportunity language.

## Intellectual Honesty Test

Pass criteria:

- Relevant conflicting or unresolved literature is acknowledged when provided or known from context.
- Discrepancies are explained through model system, assay, cell state, disease stage, cohort, sensitivity, or timing.
- Prior work is treated fairly and precisely.

Failure response:

- Add a conflict paragraph or sentence.
- Avoid rhetorical dismissal.
- Mark citation slots if references are needed but unavailable.

## Limitation Test

Pass criteria:

- Limitations are specific and scientifically consequential.
- Each limitation clarifies what the study can and cannot conclude.
- Future work is framed as a concrete test, not a vague promise.

Strong limitation examples:

- Conditional knockout is needed to establish tissue-specific causality.
- Single-cell or spatial methods are needed to resolve cell-state contributions.
- Prospective cohorts are needed before clinical stratification can be inferred.
- Direct biochemical assays are needed to confirm transient interactions.

Failure response:

- Replace generic limitations with mechanistic, technical, model, or translational boundaries.

## No-Repetition Test

Pass criteria:

- Discussion does not simply paraphrase figure-by-figure Results.
- Paragraphs synthesize evidence across experiments.
- The text adds mechanism, context, disagreement, limitation, significance, or conceptual framing.

Failure response:

- Remove figure-by-figure recaps.
- Combine repeated result statements into one interpretive claim.

## Broad Impact Test

Pass criteria:

- The closing section explains why the finding matters to the field and to adjacent scientific readers.
- Specialist details are translated into a larger conceptual implication.
- The final paragraph offers a model, framework, or future route that follows from the data.

Failure response:

- Add a broader conceptual sentence.
- Avoid vague claims such as `this study provides new insights` unless the insight is named.

## Logic Closure Test

Pass criteria:

- The Discussion answers the question promised by the Introduction.
- The study's contribution is clear relative to existing knowledge.
- The final claim is ambitious enough to matter and restrained enough to be defensible.

Failure response:

- Rewrite the opening and closing together so they form a coherent arc.
