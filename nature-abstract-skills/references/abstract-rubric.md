# Abstract Review Rubric

Use this rubric after drafting or rewriting an abstract.

## 30-Second Sniff Test

Pass criteria:

- The first sentence frames a broad scientific or translational problem without empty commonplaces.
- The second sentence defines a real frontier gap or bottleneck.
- The opening is understandable to non-specialist scientists.

Failure response:

- Replace generic background with a specific high-level problem.
- Sharpen the gap into a bottleneck, unresolved mechanism, contradiction, or limiting barrier.

## Hard Data Test

Pass criteria:

- The central result includes decisive quantitative evidence when data are available.
- Numbers are tied to the main claim, not secondary details.
- Vague phrases such as `significantly improved` are replaced with concrete endpoints when possible.

Failure response:

- Add the strongest available metric.
- If no number is available, flag the missing hard data and keep the claim conservative.

## Micro-Storyline Test

Pass criteria:

- The abstract contains background, gap, approach, key finding, mechanism, and significance.
- The approach addresses the stated gap.
- The key finding supports the mechanism.
- The mechanism leads naturally to the significance.

Failure response:

- Reorder the abstract into the six-step storyline.
- Remove disconnected claims that do not serve the central chain.

## General Reader Test

Pass criteria:

- No undefined specialist abbreviations appear.
- No figure references or citation numbers appear.
- Technical terms are minimized or briefly defined.
- The abstract can stand alone in PubMed, Google Scholar, or a journal table of contents.

Failure response:

- Expand or remove abbreviations.
- Replace protocol detail with conceptual method language.
- Delete figure and reference calls.

## Speculation And Hype Test

Pass criteria:

- The final sentence is broad but evidence-bounded.
- Clinical, commercial, therapeutic, or universal claims are supported by the provided data.
- The abstract avoids promotional adjectives.

Failure response:

- Replace hype with cautious significance.
- Bound the claim to the model, cohort, material class, disease context, or evidence type.

## Word Limit Test

Pass criteria:

- Default Science-style abstracts are 125 to 150 English words unless the user specifies another limit.
- Every sentence performs a distinct narrative function.
- No method trivia, citation padding, or repetition remains.

Failure response:

- Cut filler before cutting evidence.
- Preserve the six functions even if sentence count must be compressed.

## Final Acceptance Standard

Accept the abstract only if:

- It is stand-alone.
- It has a complete six-part story.
- It contains hard evidence when available.
- It is readable by general scientists.
- It is ambitious but not over-packaged.
