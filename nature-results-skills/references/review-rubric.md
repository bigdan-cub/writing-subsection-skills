# Results Review Rubric

Use this rubric after drafting or rewriting a Results subsection.

## Visual-Only Test

Pass criteria:

- Each main figure panel has clear labels, axes, groups, and legends.
- The reader can infer the central result from the figure without reading the full Results text.
- Images, blots, flow plots, and microscopy panels do not show obvious visual manipulation, selective contrast, suspicious cropping, or unsupported gating.
- Main figures contain enough information density to justify their space.

Failure response:

- State what the figure cannot support.
- Downgrade text claims or request missing panel information.

## Logic Chain Test

Classify the evidence as:

- Phenomenon: an observation exists.
- Association: a molecule, pathway, cell type, or feature correlates with the observation.
- Necessity: loss-of-function or inhibition reduces the phenomenon.
- Sufficiency: gain-of-function or activation induces the phenomenon.
- Mechanism: the causal route is explained.

Pass criteria:

- The Results text does not imply necessity, sufficiency, or mechanism when only association is shown.
- Rescue experiments are emphasized when present.
- Missing causal links are disclosed when central to the claim.

## Rigor Test

Check:

- Negative controls behaved as expected.
- Positive controls behaved as expected when relevant.
- Biological replicates and sample size are reported or traceable.
- Statistical tests are appropriate.
- High-throughput analyses use multiple-testing correction such as FDR or Bonferroni.
- Effect sizes and directions match the figure.

Failure response:

- Add a restrained limitation sentence.
- Avoid claiming robustness if `n`, tests, or controls are missing.

## Clutter Test

Pass criteria:

- Main Results text advances the central story.
- Long lists, routine QC, antibody validation, transfection efficiency, and minor assay checks are moved to supplementary tables or figures.
- Each paragraph has one main claim and one figure anchor.

Failure response:

- Compress procedural detail.
- Move low-level measurements to supplementary references.

## Claim Vs Data Test

Allowed claim levels:

- In vitro cell assays: claim cellular effect only.
- Animal models: claim in vivo model effect, not human efficacy.
- Patient cohort association: claim association, stratification, prognostic link, or nominated candidates.
- Perturbation plus rescue: claim requirement or mediation if the design supports it.
- Gain-of-function alone: claim sufficiency only in that model and context.

Failure response:

- Replace translational or causal overreach with a data-bounded conclusion.
- Use `suggest`, `nominated`, or `prioritized` for discovery analyses.
